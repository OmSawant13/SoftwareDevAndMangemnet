1. Primary Mathematical Formulas & Parameters

Total Effort Equation ($E$): $E = E_{\text{cleaning}} + E_{\text{views}} + E_{\text{testing}} + E_{\text{overhead}}$
Effective Daily Team Capacity ($C$): $C = \text{FTE}_{\text{BI}} (1.0) + \text{FTE}_{\text{Analyst}} (0.5) = \mathbf{1.50 \text{ Person-Days / Working Day}}$
Project Duration: $D = E / C$ (Days) • $W = D / 5$ (Weeks)
Feasibility Condition: Feasible if $W \le W_{\text{deadline}} = 8.00 \text{ Weeks}$ ($D \le 40.0 \text{ Working Days}$)

3. Step-by-Step Intermediate Calculations

Scenario A: 9-View Baseline (Target Solution)

1. View Effort: $9 \text{ views} \times 3.0 \text{ PD} = 27.0 \text{ PD}$

2. Total Effort: $8.0 (\text{Clean}) + 27.0 (\text{Views}) + 5.0 (\text{QA}) = \mathbf{40.0 \text{ PD}}$

3. Working Days: $D = 40.0 / 1.5 = \mathbf{26.67 \text{ Days}}$

4. Calendar Weeks: $W = 26.67 / 5 = \mathbf{5.33 \text{ Weeks}}$

5. Reserve Buffer: $8.00 - 5.33 = \mathbf{+2.67 \text{ Weeks (13.3 Days)}}$

✓ Healthy 33% buffer absorbs edge-case anomalies.

Scenario B: 26-Report Legacy (Why it was Rejected)

1. View Effort: $26 \text{ reports} \times 3.0 \text{ PD} = 78.0 \text{ PD}$

2. Total Effort: $8.0 (\text{Clean}) + 78.0 (\text{Views}) + 5.0 (\text{QA}) = \mathbf{91.0 \text{ PD}}$

3. Working Days: $D = 91.0 / 1.5 = \mathbf{60.67 \text{ Days}}$

4. Calendar Weeks: $W = 60.67 / 5 = \mathbf{12.13 \text{ Weeks}}$

5. Schedule Deficit: $8.00 - 12.13 = \mathbf{-4.13 \text{ Weeks (Overdue by 21 Days)}}$

✗ Misses the annual sales conference by a month.

4. Software Engineering Principles in Estimation

  • Cone of Uncertainty (Boehm & McConnell): At inception, estimation variance spans $0.25\times$ to $4.0\times$. Freezing the 9 views in Week 1 and locking the 4-tier decision table narrows uncertainty to $\pm 10\%$.
  • Brooks' Law (The Mythical Man-Month): "Adding manpower to a late software project makes it later." Introducing additional personnel midway adds communication overhead without speeding up sequential ETL data cleaning logic.
  • Parkinson's Law: Work expands to fill available time. We counteract this through weekly working prototype demos with the Sales Director every Friday.
  • Murphy's Law in Legacy Pipelines: The 2.67-week buffer explicitly protects against unexpected legacy data quirks (e.g. malformed postal codes, foreign customer addresses).
