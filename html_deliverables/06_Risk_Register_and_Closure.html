2. RMMM Plans for Top 3 Critical Project Risks

Risk 1: 14% Missing Region Data Cleaning Complexity ($RE = 20$)

Mitigation: Formulate 4-tier resolution rules in Week 1 with senior analyst; automate confidence scoring for every transaction.

Monitoring: Track daily percentage of unassigned records. If unassigned > 1.0%, trigger immediate postal directory audit.

Management (Contingency): Route remaining ambiguous records to an explicit 'Unassigned-Audit View' on dashboard.

Risk 2: Annual Sales Conference 8-Week Deadline ($RE = 20$)

Mitigation: Prune 26 legacy reports down to 9 high-value operational views, saving 51 person-days (2.67-week buffer).

Monitoring: Weekly tracking of Schedule Performance Index ($SPI$). If $SPI < 0.90$, initiate fast-tracking.

Management (Contingency): Deploy Phase 1 with top 6 views for conference stage if ETL issues arise.

Risk 3: Analyst Fear of Replacement ($RE = 16$)

Mitigation: Reposition analysts as Data Quality Governors and Anomaly Auditors, eliminating exhausting manual PDF prep.

Management: Co-present View 9 (Data Quality Console) with the analyst at the Annual Sales Conference.
