1. Introduction & Executive Summary

This Software Requirements Specification (SRS) conforms to the IEEE 830-1998 standard. It formally specifies the business requirements, user personas, functional catalog, non-functional performance baselines, and traceability matrix for the SalesLens Sales Reporting Dashboard.

Problem Statement & Justification

In the current operational state, two data analysts spend 16+ hours over weekends manually synthesizing disparate spreadsheets into 40-page PDF documents. Inconsistent calculation rules cause conflicting revenue numbers across regions, while 14% of raw billing transactions lack regional attribution. SalesLens replaces this manual overhead with an automated, role-based interactive dashboard across 9 core views within an 8-week timeline.

3. Complete Functional Requirements (FR-01 to FR-10)

Req ID Requirement Title Detailed Technical Description MoSCoW Persona
FR-01 Daily Sales Aggregation The system shall automatically ingest settled billing transactions overnight and compute total gross revenue, units sold, and invoice count by date, brand, category, and SKU. Must Have Director, Mgrs
FR-02 Target vs Actual Quotas The system shall calculate and visualize real-time variance (absolute currency and % attainment) against monthly and quarterly budgeted targets. Must Have Director, Mgrs
FR-03 Top & Bottom SKUs The system shall rank catalog SKUs by revenue contribution, margin, and volume with dynamic Top 10, Top 20, and Bottom 10 underperformer filters. Must Have Director, Analyst
FR-04 Regional Comparison Matrix The system shall provide side-by-side comparative views of all 5 geographic zones (North, South, East, West, Central) evaluating target completion and market share. Must Have Sales Director
FR-05 Regional Drill-Down The system shall allow regional managers to drill down into territory-specific distributors, cities, and sales reps with automatic RBAC filtering. Must Have Regional Mgrs
FR-06 Category & Channels The system shall visualize product category mix (Beverages, Snacks, Household) and sales channel breakdown (Modern Retail, General Trade, E-Commerce). Should Have Director, Analyst
FR-07 14% Missing Data Engine The system shall execute a deterministic 4-tier waterfall algorithm (Postal → Customer Master → Sales Rep → Audit Queue) on unassigned billing lines. Must Have Data Analyst
FR-08 Audit Console The system shall display a live log of cleaned transactions with transformation rationale, confidence scores, and manual resolution queue. Should Have Data Analyst
FR-09 Executive Briefing Export The system shall generate instant 1-page executive summary PDF/CSV briefings directly from active filter states. Should Have Sales Director
FR-10 Automated Push Digests The system shall deliver scheduled daily 07:00 AM email digests with headline revenue and quota alerts to leadership. Could Have Sales Leadership

4. Seven Measurable Non-Functional Requirements (NFRs)

NFR ID Quality Attribute Measurable Standard & Acceptance Criterion
NFR-01 Data Accuracy Reconciled revenue on dashboard must achieve ≥ 99.90% financial match with settled billing totals (maximum variance ≤ $10 per $1M billing).
NFR-02 ETL Batch Window Automated nightly data extraction, cleaning, and staging must complete in ≤ 15 minutes for 500,000 transactions before 06:00 AM daily.
NFR-03 UI Latency 95th percentile query execution latency P95 ≤ 1.50 seconds across all 9 dashboard views under standard filtering; initial app load ≤ 3.0s.
NFR-04 Security & RBAC Zero cross-territory data leakage across 10,000 automated penetration assertions. Unauthorized queries must return HTTP 403 Forbidden.
NFR-05 Availability System availability must achieve ≥ 99.50% uptime during core operational hours (06:00 to 22:00, Monday to Saturday).
NFR-06 Concurrency System must sustain ≥ 50 simultaneous active regional managers without exceeding a 10% surge in query latency.
NFR-07 Disaster Recovery Recovery Point Objective (RPO) ≤ 24 hours (automated daily snapshot); Recovery Time Objective (RTO) ≤ 60 minutes for container failover.
